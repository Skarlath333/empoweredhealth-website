Course modules

Each module has short lessons with step-by-step how-tos, decision tables, clinical pearls, red flags for referral, a case, self-check questions, references, and a printable patient handout. Free, no sign-up, at your own pace.

Module 1

Foundations of wound care

The wound care role, scope and liability, who is at risk, key terms, and how wounds heal.

3 lessons · 30 min · patient handout Module 2

Types of wounds

Pressure injury staging, diabetic, arterial and venous ulcers, moisture damage, trauma, burns and other wounds.

4 lessons · 40 min · patient handout Module 3

Assessment and diagnostic testing

History, head-to-toe skin checks, tissue types, labs, wound cultures, the ankle-brachial index, and imaging for osteomyelitis.

3 lessons · 34 min · patient handout Module 4

Dressings, products and debridement

A simple dry-versus-wet, shallow-versus-deep framework, a practical product toolkit, and choosing a debridement method.

3 lessons · 36 min · patient handout Module 5

Treatment by wound type

Step-by-step care for pressure injuries, venous and arterial ulcers, diabetic foot ulcers, skin tears, abrasions and minor burns.

4 lessons · 44 min · patient handout Module 6

Advanced wound therapies

Negative pressure wound therapy, skin substitutes and placental membranes, grafts, hyperbaric oxygen and other adjuncts.

2 lessons · 28 min · patient handout Module 7

Infection and complications

Recognizing infection, choosing antibiotics, treating osteomyelitis, fixing stalled wounds, and when to refer.

3 lessons · 34 min · patient handout Module 8

Prevention and nutrition

Repositioning, support surfaces, heel and device protection, skin care, incontinence care, nutrition, hydration and weight.

3 lessons · 36 min · patient handout Module 9

Documentation, billing and coding

Measuring and describing wounds, documentation that protects you, facility reporting, and common wound care codes.

3 lessons · 30 min · patient handout Module 10

Foot care

The diabetic foot exam, toenail care, ingrown toenails, calluses, onychomycosis, and foot care documentation.

3 lessons · 32 min · patient handout

  • Preventing pressure injuries and eating to heal
  • Questions to ask about your wound care
  • Caring for your feet

Need help with a wound or your feet? Book a visit with Empowered Health. If a wound shows signs of spreading infection, such as fever, spreading redness or confusion, get medical care right away.

This course is for general education. It is not medical advice, does not certify anyone to perform wound or foot care, does not replace clinical judgment, current guidelines or payer rules, and does not offer continuing education credit. Product names are examples, not endorsements.
