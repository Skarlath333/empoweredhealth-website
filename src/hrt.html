What is Female HRT?

Female hormone replacement therapy (female HRT) is a medical treatment that supplements or balances hormones, most commonly estrogen and progesterone, and sometimes testosterone, when the body is no longer producing optimal levels. It is most often used during perimenopause and menopause, but may also be used after hysterectomy/oophorectomy or in certain hormone deficiencies.

Here are some common reasons women might choose hormone replacement therapy:

  1. Hot flashes and night sweats
  2. Mood changes or irritability
  3. Fatigue or brain fog
  4. Sleep disturbances
  5. Weight changes or slowed metabolism
  6. Vaginal dryness or painful intercourse
  7. Low libido
  8. Bone loss/osteoporosis prevention
  9. Irregular menstrual cycles during perimenopause
