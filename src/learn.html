Root Cause Clinical Course: Functional Medicine for Providers

10 evidence-focused modules (about 5 hours) for NPs, PAs, physicians and nurses who want to bring root-cause thinking into everyday practice. Every module ends with a plain-language patient handout you can share.

Course modules

Each module has short lessons with workup tables, clinical pearls, red flags for referral, a case, self-check questions, current guideline references, and a printable patient handout. Free, no sign-up, at your own pace.
