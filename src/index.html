About us

Empowered Health

Empowered Health is a telehealth-only practice focused on treating the root causes of illness, not just symptoms. Patients receive personalized, one-on-one care with longer appointments and ongoing support tailored to their goals.

By combining traditional medicine with holistic strategies like nutrition and stress management, care plans are designed to address the whole person. The result is a more thoughtful, collaborative path to lasting health and wellness.

Featured service

Male Hormone Replacement Therapy

Male Hormone Replacement Therapy focuses on restoring healthy testosterone levels to support energy, strength, mental clarity, and overall well-being. Our approach includes a comprehensive hormone evaluation, personalized treatment plan, and ongoing monitoring to ensure safe and effective results.

Therapy may help improve symptoms such as fatigue, low libido, weight gain, reduced muscle mass, and mood changes, while supporting long-term metabolic and cardiovascular health.

Meet Your Care Team

Heath Tunnell, NP

FNP-BC, CWCN-AP, WCS-C, EDS-C, WOCN

Board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner and founder of Empowered Health.

Read bio

Heath Tunnell is a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner (FNP), Wound, Ostomy, and Continence Nurse (WOCN) board certified, and owner/provider of Empowered Health Wound and Foot Care in Knoxville, Tennessee. With specialized training from Emory University, he has been practicing for over a decade, delivering advanced, compassionate treatment to his patients.

Additionally, as the founder of Empowered Health, Heath integrates his expertise in holistic primary care, emphasizing root-cause solutions, personalized plans, and patient empowerment beyond traditional medicine. His evidence-based, approachable style has earned him outstanding reviews as one of Knoxville's top providers. He offers in-person visits in Knoxville and telemedicine across Tennessee.

When he isn't practicing, Heath enjoys exploring the Great Smoky Mountains, staying active, and spending quality time with his wife and 4 children.
