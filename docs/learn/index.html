Free course for clinicians

Root Cause Clinical Course: Functional Medicine for Providers

10 evidence-focused modules (about 5 hours) for NPs, PAs, physicians and nurses who want to bring root-cause thinking into everyday practice. Every module ends with a plain-language patient handout you can share.

Start Module 1 I'm a patient

Course modules

Each module has short lessons with workup tables, clinical pearls, red flags for referral, a case, self-check questions, current guideline references, and a printable patient handout. Free, no sign-up, at your own pace.

Module 1

Foundations of root-cause care

Systems thinking, the extended intake and timeline, and fitting functional medicine alongside conventional care.

3 lessons · 30 min · patient handout Module 2

Nutrition as therapy

Dietary patterns with real evidence, elimination diets done safely, and counseling that sticks.

4 lessons · 40 min · patient handout Module 3

GI health and the microbiome

Assessing bloating and IBS, SIBO, fiber and probiotics, and what stool testing can and can't tell you.

3 lessons · 32 min · patient handout Module 4

Insulin resistance and cardiometabolic risk

Fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, A1c, ApoB and Lp(a), plus lifestyle and medication options.

4 lessons · 42 min · patient handout Module 5

Thyroid and the fatigue workup

TSH, free T4 and antibodies, plus the iron, B12, vitamin D, sleep and mood causes of tiredness.

3 lessons · 30 min · patient handout Module 6

Perimenopause and menopause hormone therapy

Candidacy, routes and doses, risks by age and timing, and monitoring under current guidance.

3 lessons · 34 min · patient handout Module 7

Testosterone therapy in men

Diagnosis, fertility counseling, hematocrit and PSA monitoring, and when not to treat.

3 lessons · 32 min · patient handout Module 8

Stress physiology, sleep and mood

The HPA axis without the hype, CBT-I first, screening for depression and anxiety, and mind-body tools.

3 lessons · 30 min · patient handout Module 9

Environmental exposures

Endocrine disruptors, a 5-minute exposure history, and practical, low-cost counseling.

2 lessons · 20 min · patient handout Module 10

Lab selection and interpretation

Ordering with purpose, reference vs. optimal ranges, and which specialty tests have real evidence.

3 lessons · 34 min · patient handout
