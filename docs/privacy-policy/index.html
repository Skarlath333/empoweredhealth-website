Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Empowered Health ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit www.empoweredhealthtn.com.

Information we collect on this website

This website does not have user accounts and does not set advertising or tracking cookies. The only form on the site is the free course sign-up described below. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) to keep the site secure and running.

Free course sign-up

To access our free provider courses, we ask for your name, email address, phone number, mailing address and professional role. We store this information in our customer relationship management system (HubSpot) and use it to give you course access and to contact you about the courses and our services. You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing info@empoweredhealthtn.com. Please do not include any health information in the sign-up form. Your browser also remembers that you signed up (using local storage on your device) so you are not asked again.

Booking, supplements and labs

When you book an appointment, order supplements or order labs, you leave this website and use a separate service: Setmore (scheduling), Fullscript (supplements), Rupa Health or Ulta Lab Tests (labs). Information you give those services is handled under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

Email

If you email us at info@empoweredhealthtn.com, we use your message only to respond to you. Please do not send medical details by email; we will discuss your health privately during your visit.

Your health information

Health information you share as a patient is protected under applicable law, including HIPAA, and is kept in our medical records systems, not on this website.

Sale or sharing of information

We do not sell information collected through this website. Course sign-up information is shared only with the service providers that store it for us, such as HubSpot.

Children

This website is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other websites

This site links to services we do not operate. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
