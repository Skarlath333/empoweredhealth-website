How this keeps patient data private

  • Everything is encrypted (AES-256) with your passphrase and stored only in this browser. The page is blocked from sending data over the internet.
  • Use it on a computer with a login password and disk encryption (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on Mac).
  • It locks itself after a few idle minutes. Click Lock when you step away.
  • Each computer or browser has its own separate copy. To move it, download a backup and restore it on the other one.
