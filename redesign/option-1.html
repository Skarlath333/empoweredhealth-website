About us

Medicine that treats the whole person

Empowered Health is a telehealth-only practice focused on treating the root causes of illness, not just symptoms.

We combine traditional medicine with holistic strategies like nutrition and stress management, so your plan fits your life and your goals. The result is a more thoughtful, collaborative path to lasting health.

  • Longer, one-on-one appointments
  • Care plans built around your goals
  • Traditional medicine plus nutrition and stress care
  • Ongoing support, from home, anywhere in Tennessee

No. The $300 initial hormone consultation is a 60-minute visit, and labs are ordered separately.
